Quick Quiz Account Deletion

For the Quick Quiz Android app

This page explains how to request deletion of your Quick Quiz account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open Quick Quiz and go to the Profile tab.
  2. If you are signed in, note the email address on the account.
  3. Email an account deletion request to:
    pizzabrohb.commits@gmail.com
  4. Use the subject line: Quick Quiz Account Deletion Request
  5. Include:
    • The email address used for your Quick Quiz account
    • A statement that you want your account deleted

We will process account deletion requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email / Google sign-in identity for Quick Quiz)
  • Account identifiers associated with that login

What may remain

  • Flashcards and study stats stored only on your device are not automatically removed from the device by an account deletion request. You can remove them by clearing Quick Quiz app data or uninstalling the app.
  • Limited records needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance may be kept for a limited period if required by law.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can remove flashcards and local study data on your device without deleting your account by deleting sets in the app or clearing the app’s storage in Android settings.
