Quick Quiz Data Deletion

For the Quick Quiz Android app

This page explains how to request deletion of some or all of your Quick Quiz data without deleting your account.

Delete data yourself in the app

  1. Open Quick Quiz.
  2. Delete any flashcard sets you no longer want from the Library / set list.
  3. To remove local study stats and remaining on-device data:
    • Android Settings → Apps → Quick Quiz → Storage → Clear data

Request data deletion by email

  1. Email: quickbuilds10@gmail.com
  2. Subject: Quick Quiz Data Deletion Request
  3. Include:
    • The email on your Quick Quiz account
    • What you want deleted (for example: account-linked data, support emails, or all associated data except the account itself)

We will process data deletion requests within 30 days.

What can be deleted

  • Account-linked authentication records we control in Firebase, if requested and applicable
  • Support emails related to your request
  • On-device flashcards and stats (by clearing app data or deleting sets)

What may be kept

  • Your account login itself, unless you also request full account deletion
  • Limited records required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance, when required by law

Want to delete your whole account?

Use the account deletion page instead: Account deletion instructions
